ISO fits and tolerances calculator

Pick a diameter and a fit (H7/g6, H7/k6, H7/p6 or any ISO combination) and get the hole and shaft deviations, the limits of size, the maximum and minimum clearance and whether it is a clearance, transition or interference fit. Or start from the clearance you want and find the fit.

What it calculates

  • Hole and shaft deviations (ES, EI, es, ei) and limits of size, ISO 286-1 and 286-2.
  • Maximum and minimum clearance, or interference, and the type of fit: clearance, transition, interference.
  • 13 recommended hole-basis fits and 8 shaft-basis fits, or any combination: holes C–S, shafts c–s, grades IT5–IT11, sizes up to 500 mm.
  • Reverse search: from the clearance you want to the fits that give it, hole basis or shaft basis.
  • Suggested machining and roughness Ra, the drawing callout, and a comparison table of the fits of the same system.

Method

The hole and the shaft each have a tolerance zone: its width is the standard tolerance IT of the grade, its position is the fundamental deviation of the letter for the diameter range. The clearance is the hole size minus the shaft size: its maximum and minimum follow from the deviations.

Worked example

Example: a hub on a 30 mm shaft with an H7/k6 fit (light press fit, with a key).

The ISO 286 tables in the calculator are checked by 904 tests: about 50 published deviations, the Δ rule on every range and the growth of IT with grade.

Questions

How do I read H7/g6?
The capital letter and number are the hole, the lower case ones the shaft. The letter sets the position of the tolerance zone (H: lower deviation zero, g: a little below the nominal size), the number is the IT grade, i.e. the width of the zone.
Hole basis or shaft basis?
Hole basis (H…) is the usual choice because holes are made with fixed-size tools (reamers, gauges); shaft basis (…h) is used when one drawn shaft carries several parts with different fits.
Which fit for a bearing, a gear hub, a sliding pin?
As a first orientation: sliding pins and guides H7/g6 or H7/h6, hubs with a key H7/k6, press fits H7/p6 to H7/s6. For rolling bearings follow the bearing manufacturer's tables, because the bearing bore has its own tolerance.
Can it calculate the force or pressure of a press fit?
Not in this station: the interference fit can be passed to the shrink fit station D-07, which gives the contact pressure, the transmissible torque and the stresses.
