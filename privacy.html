Privacy

Torsio has no accounts and does not ask for personal data. The calculations run in your browser and stay there: the site only receives anonymous statistics on the pages visited.

Your calculations

All calculations, reports, PDFs and DXF files are produced by your browser. The values you enter are not sent to any server.

  • Shareable links: the values of the calculation are written in the address of the link. Whoever receives the link sees them, as does anyone who sees it in the browser history.
  • Project, session and language: the project data (name, job number, client, revisions, logo), the last values entered in each module (so you find them again when you reload the page; “Start from scratch” deletes them) and the chosen language are saved in the browser's local storage (localStorage), only on your device. You delete them by clearing the site data in the browser.
  • Project files: the .torsio.json file is saved to and opened from your computer; the site keeps no copy.

Visit statistics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and only provides aggregated data. For every page view it records: the page address (without parameters), the referring site, country and region, device type, operating system and browser. The visitor is recognised by an anonymous fingerprint of the request, discarded after 24 hours.

In the calculator it also counts which module you open (for example /app/shafts), when you open a report or download a PDF, HTML or DXF (for example /pdf/shafts, only the module name) and the ✓ / ✕ votes on the “Was it useful?” button. Before sending, a filter removes all parameters from the address, except those telling which link you came from (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign): the values of the calculations never reach the statistics.

Feedback and contact

The feedback and contact form is provided by Tally (Tally BV, Belgium; data stored in Europe). It loads only when you press ✓ / ✕ or “Contact”, not before. It receives the vote, the module name and the language, plus whatever you choose to write: every question is optional, the email too.

Cookies, fonts and external services

The site uses no cookies, neither its own nor third-party ones. Fonts (Archivo, IBM Plex Mono), formulas (KaTeX) and styles are served by the site itself: opening the pages sends no request to Google or other services. The site is hosted by Vercel, whose servers, like those of any site, receive the IP address of visitors for technical reasons.
