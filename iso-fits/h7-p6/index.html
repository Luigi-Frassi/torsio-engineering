When to use it

Permanent press or heat shrink fit.

It is an interference fit: on every size the shaft is larger than the hole, so the parts are pressed or shrunk together and the fit itself can carry torque.

Indicative roughness: hole Ra 0.8-1.6 µm, shaft Ra 0.4-0.8 µm. Typical machining: shaft by precision cylindrical grinding, hole by internal grinding or fine reaming.

On the drawing: Ø30 H7/p6 on the assembly, Ø30 H7 on the hub and Ø30 p6 on the shaft (or the limits of size).

Questions

Is H7/p6 a clearance or an interference fit?
Interference. It is an interference fit: on every size the shaft is larger than the hole, so the parts are pressed or shrunk together and the fit itself can carry torque.
What clearance does H7/p6 give on 50 mm?
From −42 to −1 µm (range over 30 up to 50 mm; negative values are interference).
And on 100 mm?
From −59 to −2 µm (range over 80 up to 120 mm).
Where do these values come from?
From the ISO 286-1 and 286-2 tables built into the Torsio calculator, checked by 904 tests against published deviations. For a contract drawing always refer to the standard.
