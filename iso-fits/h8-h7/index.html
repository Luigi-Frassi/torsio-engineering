When to use it

Sliding location, medium precision.

It is a clearance fit: on every size the hole is larger than the shaft, so the parts slide or turn.

Indicative roughness: hole Ra 1.6 µm, shaft Ra 0.8-1.6 µm.

On the drawing: Ø30 H8/h7 on the assembly, Ø30 H8 on the hub and Ø30 h7 on the shaft (or the limits of size).

Questions

Is H8/h7 a clearance or an interference fit?
Clearance. It is a clearance fit: on every size the hole is larger than the shaft, so the parts slide or turn.
What clearance does H8/h7 give on 50 mm?
From 0 to +64 µm (range over 30 up to 50 mm; negative values are interference).
And on 100 mm?
From 0 to +89 µm (range over 80 up to 120 mm).
Where do these values come from?
From the ISO 286-1 and 286-2 tables built into the Torsio calculator, checked by 904 tests against published deviations. For a contract drawing always refer to the standard.
